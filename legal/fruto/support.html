Common Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open frúto → Settings → "Manage or Cancel Subscription", or go to your Apple ID settings → Subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

On the paywall or in Settings, tap Restore Purchase while signed into the same Apple ID.

How do I report inappropriate content?

Tap the small flag icon next to any community recipe, comment, or sighting — or use Settings → Report a Problem. We review all reports and remove content that violates our guidelines.

A price looks wrong — can I fix it?

Yes! Report the correct price from the Prices tab, or scan your receipt. The freshest community reports are shown first. You can also flag bad data via Settings → Report a Problem.

Why don't I see prices for my store?

Prices come from the community — if nobody has reported prices at your store yet, be the first! Scan a receipt to add prices for everything you bought at once.
